# Exploratory-Data-Analysis-EDA :
 Summary of EDA Observations – Titanic Dataset
Survival Rate: Most passengers did not survive; survival rate was below 40%.

Gender Impact: Females had a significantly higher survival rate than males, indicating gender influenced rescue priorities.

Class Influence: 1st-class passengers had the highest chance of survival, showing that socioeconomic status played a key role.

Age Distribution: Most passengers were between 20 and 40 years old. Survivors tended to be slightly younger.

Travel Companions: Majority of passengers had either no or only one sibling/spouse with them.

Fare Patterns: Most passengers paid low fares. Survivors generally paid higher fares, hinting at their better accommodation (higher class).

Age vs Fare (Survival): Younger passengers who paid higher fares were more likely to survive. Non-survivors were scattered across all fare levels.

Missing Data: Age was imputed based on passenger class; Cabin column was dropped due to too many missing values.
